## Quick answer

Business name is simpler. Limited company is more formal. The right choice depends on your stage and goals.

## A simple comparison

### Business Name
- simpler
- faster to understand
- good for small and early-stage businesses

### Limited Company
- more formal
- stronger identity
- better for scale and partnerships
