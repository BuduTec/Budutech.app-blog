## Quick answer

A business name registration is the simplest CAC structure for an individual or small team that wants to operate under a formal business identity.

## Why people choose it

- It is usually simpler than incorporation
- It helps a business look more official
- It is a practical starting point for new founders

## FAQ

### Can I register from anywhere in Nigeria?

Yes. The process can be handled remotely.

### Is a business name the same as a limited company?

No. They are different structures.
