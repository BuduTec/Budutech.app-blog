## Quick answer

Processing time depends on the type of registration and how quickly the necessary stages are completed.

## What can slow it down

- incorrect details
- missing documents
- name issues
- choosing the wrong structure

## FAQ

### Is a business name faster than an NGO?

Usually yes.

### Can I speed it up?

You can reduce delays by submitting correct information early.
