## Quick answer

A limited liability company is a more formal business structure used by founders who need stronger corporate positioning and a clearer ownership structure.

## Why founders choose it

- stronger corporate image
- clearer business identity
- better fit for growth
- useful for partnerships

## FAQ

### Is company registration always better?

No. It depends on your goals.

### Does it take longer?

Usually yes, because it is more detailed.
